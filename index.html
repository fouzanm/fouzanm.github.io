01 Retail & POS · Odoo 19

POS Day-Close & Inventory Control

Odoo 1914 outletsPOS (OWL)InventorySpreadsheet dashboards

The need

A Bahrain-based retailer with 14 outlets that makes its own products needed every outlet to reconcile sales, cash, stock and leftovers at the end of each day.

What I built

  • A business-day workflow on top of POS shifts: a branch opens a day, runs several cashier sessions, and cannot close the day while any session is still open.
  • One-step day close that posts session, payment and bank-statement entries and consolidates orders, discounts and net sales.
  • Stock counting inside the POS screen: staff enter counted quantities, and a confirmed summary creates the stock adjustments automatically.
  • Leftover handling that splits stock into "can be sold" and "to be wasted", with journal entries and inter-company transfers, plus hourly-sales and leftover dashboards.

Outcome

Each day closes in a controlled sequence: sessions, cash, stock and leftovers are reconciled before the day can be marked closed.

02 Finance · Odoo 18

Xero Integration for a New Zealand Client

Odoo 18Xero APIOAuth 2.0WebhooksNew Zealand

The need

A New Zealand company running Odoo 18 needed its invoices, payments and taxes to stay in step with Xero instead of being re-entered by hand.

What I built

  • Adapted a commercial Xero connector (OAuth 2.0, REST API) to the client's Odoo 18 setup and extended it for their workflow.
  • Fixed the invoice export to Xero for invoices with multiple lines.
  • Added a payment webhook so payment updates sync automatically, and updated the tax views on sales orders and invoices.
  • Delivered the client's other Odoo 18 customizations alongside it: analytic accounts, helpdesk, sales extensions, custom sales reports and a custom backend theme.

Outcome

Invoices, payments and tax details move between Odoo and Xero without manual re-entry, and each sync is logged so problems can be traced.

03 Sales automation · Odoo 18

Sale Orders from PDF Documents

Odoo 18PythonpdfplumberWizardsConfigurable mapping

The need

A client that received customer orders as PDF files wanted sale orders created from them without re-typing every line.

What I built

  • Create sale orders directly from PDF attachments on project tasks, reading the PDF text with pdfplumber.
  • A configurable keyword-to-field mapping, so each customer's PDF layout can be matched to sale order fields and order lines without writing code.
  • Options to create missing customers and products automatically, with default customer, default product and date-format settings.
  • A guided wizard, a digitization menu and a smart button on each task that lists the sale orders created from it, plus a Dutch translation.

Outcome

Orders that arrive as PDFs turn into sale orders from the project task, and new PDF layouts are handled through configuration instead of new code.

04 Service · Odoo 18 Enterprise

After-Sales Repair & Service Management

Odoo 18 EERepairQualityApprovalsMulti-company

The need

A multi-company group handling repairs and warranty service needed one controlled flow from service request to repair, with approvals and credit checks built in.

What I built

  • A service request lifecycle with warranty auto-identification, assessment and routing, then one-click conversion to a repair order.
  • Guided wizards for rescheduling, spare-parts consumption, quotations and resolution, with scheduled notifications.
  • Quality inspection on repairs, a credit application form with multi-stage approval, and configurable approval rules for sales, purchases and repair estimates.

05 Trading & Logistics · Odoo 17

Trading Controls, Landed Cost & Fleet Maintenance

Odoo 17Landed costFleetMaintenanceCredit control

The need

A trading company with a vehicle fleet and imported goods needed to protect its margins and allocate landed costs accurately.

What I built

  • Automatic customer freeze driven by overdue invoices, so credit rules are enforced without manual follow-up.
  • Delivery blocked when the selling price is below item cost, with return and transfer restrictions by user group.
  • Partial landed cost allocation and bill adjustment, so import costs reach stock valuation correctly.
  • Maintenance linked to accounting assets, with cost tracking, stage approvals, material requests and maintenance order costing for the fleet.

Outcome

Credit and pricing rules are enforced by the system instead of by follow-up, and import and maintenance costs flow into the accounts.
