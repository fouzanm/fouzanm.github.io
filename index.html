01 Retail & POS · Odoo 19

POS Day-Close & Inventory Control

The client A multi-branch retailer that produces goods in-house and sells them through its own POS outlets, and needed every branch to reconcile sales, cash, stock and leftovers at the end of each day.

Odoo 19POS (OWL)InventorySpreadsheet dashboardsMulti-company

What I built

  • A day-session workflow on top of POS shifts: a branch opens a business day, runs several cashier sessions, and cannot close the day while any session is still open.
  • One-step day close that posts the session, payment and bank-statement entries and consolidates orders, payments, discounts and net sales into a single day-close record.
  • Inventory closing inside the POS screen: staff enter counted quantities, see the differences, and a confirmed summary creates the stock adjustments automatically.
  • Leftover handling that splits remaining stock into "can be sold" and "to be wasted", with journal entries, stock moves and inter-company transfers through a transit location.
  • Spreadsheet dashboards for hourly sales with peak-hour detection, leftover waste and recovery, and product mix, plus day-close, shift-close and stock register printouts.
  • Controls around the till: stock and payment shortage tracking, bank charges on POS payments, and blocking orders paid below the bill amount.

Odoo 18 EERepairQualityApprovalsMulti-company
